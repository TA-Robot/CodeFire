Status: active manager admission gate  
Owner: portfolio manager

## Why This Gate Exists

The interrupted retroactive pilot has a valid, digest-bound calibration freeze
but its intervention set was selected from the provisional root-cause audit.
Provider capacity returning does not prove that those interventions target the
actual bottleneck. Resuming solely because Claude is available would spend a
scarce provider window before review omission, repair-introduced defects,
repair-contract failure, oracle weakness, evidence/routing behavior, and
unavailable evidence have been independently separated.

This gate preserves the frozen work without treating sunk calibration cost as
a reason to finish an irrelevant experiment.

## Prerequisites Before Resume Or Retirement

The manager first requires:

1. the committed Fugu blind-calibration report, normalized generation-chain
   effect-episode and observation-row identity digests, and proof that its
   pre-audit snapshot excluded the provisional and temporal reports;
2. the committed fresh-session Fugu episode/row reconciliation, per-axis
   comparison, comparison freeze, and unchanged temporal check;
3. the committed Claude adjudication of every decision-relevant episode
   identity/closure or row/axis disagreement, reproduced production effect,
   routed decision-critical unavailable value, policy/contradiction row, and
   denominator-changing split/merge;
4. aggregate sensitivity against both immutable source classifications;
5. a mapping from pilot interventions I1/I3/I4/I5 to the adjudicated causal
   classes they could actually test; and
6. a verified Claude health probe and admission evidence.

The three exact pending product/control-plane review resumes retain downstream
release priority in a constrained Claude window. The pilot is not promoted
ahead of Cargo `4e5c0e7`, Upload `446cf4a`, or KGI/KPI `753c8db` merely
because its prior calibration consumed time or money.

## Intervention-Relevance Matrix

| Frozen pilot intervention | Resume relevance requires | Does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| I1 predecessor re-derivation | adjudicated pre-existing effects whose exact prior scope/detectability remains decision-relevant | absolute review recall |
| I3 repair-delta sweep | adjudicated repair-introduced effects plus exact pre-repair hazard/oracle coverage; uncovered hazards only justify testing a blind premortem, not adopting one | one universal line-count threshold or hindsight proof that a premortem would predict the effect |
| I4 E1–E9 oracle admissibility | adjudicated oracle failures or closure claims that survive deliberate falsification | a nine-item mandatory checklist |
| I5 evidence/routing separation | adjudicated evidence-only, policy-promoted, contradictory, or non-blocking routing rows | permission to weaken integration authority |

Multiple rows may support multiple interventions. A row count alone cannot
make an intervention relevant. Repeated observations of one still-open effect
do not become independent evidence for an intervention; compare
observation-, unique-episode-, and generation-level results.

## Decision

After prerequisites, choose exactly one:

### `RESUME_UNCHANGED`

Allowed only when at least one intervention remains decision-relevant and the
frozen definitions, eligibility rules, and outcome measures can answer the
question without relabelling calibration. Resume the same Claude session,
verify both preserved digests, keep calibration immutable, and open holdouts
in the preregistered order.

### `RESUME_WITH_SEPARATE_SENSITIVITY_REPLAY`

Allowed when the frozen pilot remains interpretable but adjudication changes
one or more causal labels, episode links, or denominators. Finish the frozen
holdout protocol unchanged first. Then run a separately identified replay
over only the affected rows/episodes; do not edit the frozen calibration or
collapse the two analyses.

### `RETIRE_PRESERVED_PILOT`

Required when no tested intervention maps to an adjudicated decision-relevant
failure mode, or when the adjudication invalidates a frozen definition enough
that its holdout score cannot answer the intended question. Keep the freeze,
event log, incident, worktree, and session identity as historical evidence;
do not open holdouts merely to obtain a terminal report.

### `UNAVAILABLE`

Use when calibration/comparison/adjudication evidence is insufficient to
choose among the above. Do not resume by default.

## Process-Change Boundary

Completing a retroactive pilot can only return evidence for a later bounded
live experiment with a preregistered eligible cohort and stopping rule. It
cannot adopt:

- a finding quota;
- a checklist length;
- a template shape or mandatory detail count;
- a mandatory repair premortem;
- a repair-delta threshold;
- a severity or routing policy; or
- a universal review method.

Any live experiment must preregister a comparable baseline and report later
finding capture, noise, duplication, false positives, author burden, reviewer
burden, provider cost, elapsed queue/service time, rework generations, and
trusted-integration outcome. A field is retained only when it maps to an
adjudicated failure mode and adds measured signal at acceptable burden.

## Required Manager Record

Before any pilot continuation, append one dated decision record naming:

- classifier, comparator, and adjudication commits/digests;
- the exact supported causal classes;
- observation-, effect-episode-, and generation-level sensitivity;
- per-intervention relevance;
- selected decision above and falsifier;
- product-review priority/admission evidence;
- preserved freeze/event-log digest verification; and
- whether a separate sensitivity replay is required.
